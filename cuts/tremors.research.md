## What fans come for

- **Burt & Heather's basement arsenal**: "Broke into the wrong goddamn rec room, didn't you, you bastard!" The most-quoted scene in the film.
- **Graboid naming**: Walter Chang names them "Graboids".
- **"Running's not a plan. Running's what you do once a plan fails."**
- **"We killed it… we killed that motherhumper!"** The first kill, at the culvert.
- **The tongues reveal**: the "snakes" are only the tongues of something huge.
- **Pole-vaulting across the boulders.**
- **Burt: "Food for five years… underground goddamn monsters."**
- **"My cannon."** Then fishing with pipe bombs.
- **Ending**: Stumpy is driven over the cliff. The opening shot of Val peeing off that same cliff sets it up. "Stampede" pays off Earl's opening stampede story.
- **Heather Gummer is Reba McEntire.** It's a well-known casting fact, and our running "is it Reba?" argument pays off at the credits.

## Our running dynamic

- **The Reba debate** runs from 1502 to 5810: "Reba was in this" / "That is not Reba" / "you owe me an apology" / "now I'm on the fence" / "It's her for sure", then "I stand corrected" at the credits. It's the cut's through-line.
- **Biggest reaction peak:** the basement fight, 3818–3831 ("Wow. They have a lot of guns" → "Oh, no, no, no, no, no, no" → "Oh my gosh, holy crap").
