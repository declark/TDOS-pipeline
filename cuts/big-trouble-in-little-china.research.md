## What fans come for

- **"It's all in the reflexes"**: the film's catchphrase, set up on the bottle trick and paid off when Jack catches Lo Pan's knife and kills him with it.
- **The Pork Chop Express CB opener** ("Have you paid your dues, Jack?" / "The check is in the mail"), and its bookend at the end.
- **The alley battle**: Chang Sing vs Wing Kong, then the Three Storms, then Lo Pan with light pouring out of his mouth; named by every list. Jack drives straight through him.
- **Lo Pan (James Hong)**: the old man in the wheelchair ("Shut up, Mr. Burton!", "Indeed!") and the floating young spirit; one of the film's main draws.
- **Jack as the sidekick who thinks he's the hero**: the joke of the whole film. Peaks when he fires into the ceiling at the wedding and knocks himself out, then faces Lo Pan with Gracie's lipstick all over his mouth.
- **Wang Chi as the real action hero**: his mid-air sword fight with Rain.
- **Egg Shen's potion and the six-demon bag**: "huge buzz", "I feel kind of invincible", "Is it getting hot in here or is it just me?".
- **The Three Storms**: Thunder inflating until he explodes is the best-remembered death; Lightning crushed under a statue.
- **"Call the president"** and **"I was born ready"**: the quoted Jack lines from the middle of the film.
- **The creature effects**: the Guardian (the floating ball of eyeballs) and the Wild Man, who turns up on the back of Jack's truck in the last shot.
- **Trivia viewers know**: a flop that became a cult classic; an influence on Mortal Kombat (we bring this up ourselves at 1327–1338 and the flop at 5842).
