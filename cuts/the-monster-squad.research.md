# The Monster Squad (1987) — research & beat sheet

Offset: film starts at **257.25s** in the reaction recording (`Watch The Monster Squad.mov`).
Times below are **recording time** (film time + 257.25).

## Transcript notes

- The default VAD transcript of the film (`The Monster Squad (1987).words.json`) is empty over the
  prologue (film 0–410s) and most of the climax (film 3583–4081s, 4275–4572s): the VAD filter treated
  dialogue under the score as non-speech. A second pass without VAD was run to
  `The Monster Squad (1987).novad.words.json` / `Watch The Monster Squad.novad.words.json` and merged in
  only where the VAD transcript has nothing. It recovered "Give me the amulet, you bitch" (heard as
  "ambulance"), Lisa's "you're not a virgin, are you", "I will have your son", "See you later, band-aid
  breath".
- "Wolfman's got nards!" appears only in the reaction mic transcript (3591.1); the film pass heard it as
  "Man's got no hearts". Same moment either way.
- The prologue still has no film dialogue in either pass; it's anchored on our lines and beat edges.
- The reaction mic picks up the film audio, so film lines echo into our transcript ~1–3s late. Those
  echoes were filtered out before picking our commentary.

## What fans come for

- **"Wolfman's got nards!"** The film's signature line, and the title of the 2018 documentary.
- **Horace**: "My name's Horace" (bullied at school) pays off when he shotguns the Gill-man ("My name is Horace!").
- **Phoebe & Frankenstein's monster**: the friendship, and his goodbye at the vortex.
- **Dracula: "Give me the amulet, you bitch!"** to a five-year-old.
- **Garlic pizza** vs. Dracula; **"Creatures stole my Twinkie"**; **"Wolfman cannot drive a car / wolf dork"**.
- **Scary German Guy**: the Van Helsing translation, and the quiet reveal of his camp tattoo.
- **Lisa isn't a virgin**, so Phoebe reads the incantation.
- **Ending**: the Army shows up late, and Sean hands over the "Monster Squad" business card.
