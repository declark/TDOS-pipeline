## What fans come for

- **Cooper refuses to shoot the dog**: the opening test that sets Cooper as the hero and Ryan as the villain, and pays off the title.
- **Campfire banter → the dead cow**: "Spiders. And women. Spider women", Wells' Eddie Oswald tattoo story, then a gutted cow lands on them — the film's first big scare, cited by multiple reviews.
- **"I am not breaking radio silence because you lot got spooked by a dead flying fucking cow"**: Wells' most-quoted line.
- **The slaughtered SAS camp and "There was only supposed to be one"**: Ryan's slip that sets up the conspiracy.
- **The woods chase / Wells gutted / Bruce impaled**: the shoot-and-scoot sequence reviewers single out.
- **Wells' guts and "Sausages"**: superglue and whiskey surgery, Sam the dog going for the intestines — the film's signature gross-out comedy.
- **"Lycanthropes" — Megan explains the werewolves**: full moon, silver, "the eyebrows thing is nonsense".
- **The siege**: pots, pans and boiling water, the first assault on the house, "Dogs. More like pussies."
- **Ryan's confession — "You were bait"**: the squad were expendable; Ryan then turns.
- **Megan's reveal — "I'm the real thing"**: the zoologist is one of the Uath family and opens the door.
- **Spoon's kitchen fight — "I hope I give you the shits"**: Spoon boxes a werewolf with pans and fists; his last line is one of the most loved in the film.
- **"We go together or we don't fucking go" / Wells blows the house**: Wells, turning, sends Cooper to the cellar and ignites the gas.
- **Silver letter opener payoff**: the gift from the opening ("No knight should be without his sword") kills Ryan; "You tried licking your own balls yet? ... You don't fucking have any."
- **"Werewolves ate my platoon!"**: tabloid ending under "England 5 Germany 1".
- Trivia viewers know: Kevin McKidd (Grey's Anatomy) as Cooper, Sean Pertwee as Wells, Liam Cunningham as Ryan; Neil Marshall's debut before The Descent; shot in Luxembourg for £2.3m; Statham was first cast as Cooper; Simon Pegg turned down Spoon.

## Our running dynamic

- **"The Thing + American Werewolf had a baby"**: Teresa's pitch in the intro (78–94), checked during the watch ("Just like the thing" 375) and paid off in the verdict (6297–6320: "They were gonna kill a dog in the beginning just like The Thing ... in the middle of nowhere").
- **The letter opener is "the sword"**: running joke from the gift (208 "I thought it was a sword") through 253 "Where's the sword now? Get the sword", 2922–2949 "there's a real sword", 4969 "he took the sword with him", to the payoff 6156.
- **"Dog people"**: our name for the werewolves (3020, 3105, 3976, 6340 "the dog people were bizarre").
- **Prediction: Ryan turns** — called early and repeatedly (2731 "that dude's gonna turn into a dog person", 3020, 3386 "He's healed", 3441, 3976), pays off 4847 / 4939.
- **Prediction: Megan knows Ryan / is part of it** — 2193 "she knows him for sure", 4052 "I told you she knew him", 4140 "She knows more than she's saying", 4568 "Those are her family members", then the who-said-it-first squabble 4993–5029 ("I said that already" / "No, you didn't"); pays off at the reveal 5430–5526.
- **Verdict split**: one of us liked it ("I thought it was pretty cool" 6329, "that dude fighting at the end whacking with a pot ... boxing the dog" 6367–6375), the other found it "cheesy" (6348–6359) and was thrown by Megan changing on demand (6386–6407).
