## What fans come for

- **The trunk.** Whoever opens the Malibu's trunk is vaporised in white light, leaving their boots. It happens to the motorcycle cop in the cold open and to Archie later.
- **The flying Malibu.** Miller drives the glowing green car up over LA and into space, with Otto aboard.
- **Bud (Harry Dean Stanton) and his Repo Code**, plus "Ordinary fucking people… I hate 'em" and "The life of a repo man is always intense".
- **Miller's "lattice of coincidence"**: plate of shrimp, flying saucers that are really time machines, "the more you drive, the less intelligent you are".
- **The generic products.** FOOD, DRINK and BEER labels, the stoned parents and the televangelist.
- **Punk side-plot.** Duke's "I blame society" death, plus "Let's go get sushi and not pay".
- **Plettschner** ("Don't ever say fuck you to me") and **Lite** ("I'm still on the job, white boy").

### Notes

- Bud's "The life of a repo man is always intense" is badly transcribed in the film transcript: it sits inside a 30s mis-timed segment (R1432–1462), and the clean word is at R1464 ("People, man's always intense"). It's audible in the reaction track at 1469.
- Miller's version of the line in the finale isn't in the film transcript. Our track picks it up at ~5388.
- Several stretches have no film dialogue in the transcript (R3958–4111, R4230–4335, R5282–5410). Those beats are anchored on the beat edges.
